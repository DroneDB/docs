## When to use it

You uploaded an orthophoto and want a customer or a colleague to see it. They do not need an
account. You send them a link, or you put the map in your web page.

## Who can see a dataset

Each dataset has a **visibility**:

| Visibility | Who can see and download the data |
|---|---|
| **Private** (the default) | Only you and the people in your organization. |
| **Unlisted** | Anybody who has the link. The dataset does not show in lists or searches. |
| **Public** | Everybody. People can also find the dataset without the link. |

To share a map with people outside your organization, the dataset must be **Unlisted** or
**Public**. For a customer, **Unlisted** is usually correct.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open your dataset on the **Files** tab and click the orthophoto once to select it.

2. Right-click the orthophoto and click **Share/Embed**. You can also click the share button at
   the top right of the page.

3. If the dataset is private, the **Share / Embed** window shows
   **Allow access to anyone with the link**. Click it. This makes the dataset **Unlisted**.

   <Screenshot id="get-started/share-first-map--private" />

4. In the **Share** list, keep **Link**. Click **Copy to clipboard** next to the
   **Share link** and send the link. It opens the orthophoto on the map.

   <Screenshot id="get-started/share-first-map--link" />

5. To show the map in a web page, select **Embed** in the **Share** list. Copy the code and
   paste it into the HTML of your page. The code is an `iframe` of 600 × 400 pixels; change
   `width` and `height` in the code to change its size.

   The embedded map shows the orthophoto without the Hub header. Visitors can zoom and pan.

</Steps>

## Change the visibility later

<Steps>

1. Open the dataset and click **Settings** (the wrench at the top right).

2. In **Sharing**, select **Private**, **Unlisted** or **Public** in **Visibility**. Hub saves
   the change at once.

   <Screenshot id="get-started/share-first-map--settings" />

</Steps>

When you make a dataset private again, links and embeds stop working for people outside your
organization.

## Try it on a public dataset
