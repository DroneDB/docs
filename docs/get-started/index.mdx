DroneDB Hub is a web application for drone data. You upload your files, and Hub shows them on a
map or in 3D in your browser. You then measure, analyse and share them with a link. You do not
install anything.

<TryLive org="odm" ds="brighton-beach" label="Explore a dataset on Hub"
         hint="A public survey of Brighton Beach. You do not need an account." />

## What Hub does with your data

- **Stores** your survey: photos, orthophotos, elevation models, point clouds, 3D models,
  Gaussian splats, vector files, videos, panoramas and documents, in folders you choose.
- **Shows** each file in the correct viewer: a map, a point cloud viewer, a 3D model viewer, a
  splat viewer or a 360° panorama. Large files open fast, because Hub prepares a streaming copy
  of them after the upload.
- **Measures and analyses**: distances, areas and volumes, elevation profiles, contour lines,
  plant health indices and thermal values.
- **Shares**: you decide who sees a dataset. Send a link, embed a map in your web page, or open
  the data in QGIS.

Ordinary cloud storage keeps your files, but it cannot show an orthophoto on a map or a point
cloud in 3D. Hub can.

Hub does not make orthophotos from your photos by itself. For that, use
[photogrammetry](/hub-guide/processing/photogrammetry.mdx) on Hub, or process the photos in
[WebODM](https://docs.webodm.org) and upload the results.

## Who uses it

Surveyors, farmers, inspectors, GIS analysts and drone pilots who want to check a survey, keep
it in one place and show it to a customer or a colleague.

:::tip Run your own Hub

DroneDB Hub runs on [hub.dronedb.app](https://hub.dronedb.app). You can also run the same
software on your own server: see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting). Plans and prices are on
[dronedb.app](https://dronedb.app/plans).

:::
