You need an account to upload data. With an account you get your own organization, where you
create datasets. This page takes about 3 minutes.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Register.** Go to [dronedb.app/register](https://dronedb.app/register/). Type a
   **Username**, your **E-mail** and a **Password**. The username can have lowercase letters,
   numbers, dashes and underscores. Accept the terms and click **Register**.

   Plans, the free trial and prices are on [dronedb.app/plans](https://dronedb.app/plans).

2. **Sign in to Hub.** Go to [hub.dronedb.app](https://hub.dronedb.app). Type the same
   username and password, and click **Login**. Hub opens the **Organizations** page.

   <Screenshot id="get-started/first-dataset--sign-in" />

3. **Open your organization.** Click the user icon at the top right, then click
   **My Datasets**. This is the page of your personal organization. It has the same name as
   your username, and only you can see it.

   <Screenshot id="get-started/first-dataset--my-datasets" />

4. **Create a dataset.** Click **Create Dataset**. In the **Create new dataset** window:

   - Type a **Dataset Name**, for example `Quarry survey 2026`. Hub makes the
     **Dataset Slug** from the name. The slug is the part of the dataset address that comes
     after your organization, for example `/r/<username>/quarry-survey-2026`. If you rename
     the dataset later, the slug changes too, and links to the old address stop working.
   - Keep **Visibility** at **Private** for now. You can change it at any time.
   - Optionally, type a **Tagline**: a short description that shows in the list of datasets.
   - Keep **Open dataset after creation** selected.

   Click **Create**.

5. **Your dataset is open.** It is empty. Continue with [Upload your data](/get-started/upload.mdx).

   <Screenshot id="get-started/first-dataset--empty-dataset" />

</Steps>

:::tip Quick upload without a dataset

**Upload Files** in the user menu makes a new dataset for you. Hub puts the dataset in your
personal organization, uploads the files to it and opens it.

:::

## Limits & requirements

- The storage of your account depends on your plan. The user menu shows how much you use. See
  [Plans & limits](/hub-guide/account/plans.mdx).
- A dataset name must give a slug that no other dataset in the organization has. If it does,
  the window shows an error and **Create** stays off.
