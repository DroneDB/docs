## When to use it

An **organization** groups datasets, and a **dataset** holds files. Use the **Organizations** page to find
the organizations you work in. Use the datasets page of an organization to find a dataset and open it.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Sign in and click **Organizations** in the header (on a server with one organization the link is **Datasets**).
   Hub lists the organizations that you own, that you are a member of, or that are public.
   **My Datasets** in the user menu (your user name, top right) opens the datasets of your personal organization.
   The header also shows where you are, as a breadcrumb: **Organizations** / the organization / the dataset. Click a name to go back to
   it. A visitor who may not open the organization sees its slug, not a link.

   <Screenshot id="datasets/browse--organizations" />

2. To see every organization that you can open, turn on **Show all**. For a server
   administrator, this lists every organization of the server.
3. Type in **Search organizations** to filter the list by name, slug, description or owner. The x button
   (**Clear search**) or Esc empties the field. When nothing matches, the list says **No organizations found**
   and **No organizations match "your text"**, with a **Clear search** button.
4. Use the sort control to sort by **Name**, **Creation date**, **Last modified** or
   **Number of datasets**. The arrow button next to it changes the direction.
5. Click an organization to open its datasets page.
6. On the datasets page, click a column header to sort by **Dataset**, **Creation Date**, **Modified**,
   **Files**, **Size** or **Visibility**. Type in **Search datasets** to filter by name, slug or tagline: the list and the **Map** both show what matches, and the
   number of datasets of the organization in the header does not change with the search (under the list, a search says for example **Showing 1 to 2 of 2 datasets, 5 in the organization**). An
   organization with no datasets says so and, if you can write, offers **Create dataset**, **Import** and
   **Upload**.
7. Click **Map** to see one pin per dataset. Click a cluster to zoom in, and click a pin to open the
   dataset.

   <Screenshot id="datasets/browse--datasets-map" />

8. Click a dataset row to open the dataset in the [file browser](/hub-guide/files/browser.mdx).

</Steps>

You do not need an account to open a public organization. Open its address directly, for example
`hub.dronedb.app/r/odm`. Hub then shows its public datasets. A signed-in user who is not a member
sees a public organization in the list and opens it read-only: there is no **Create Dataset** or
**Import**.

## Options

| Option | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **List** / **Map** | Shows the organizations or datasets as a list, or as pins on a map | List |
| **Show all** | Lists every organization that you can open (every organization, for a server administrator) | Off |
| Sort control | Sorts the organizations | Name, ascending |
| **No location** | On the map: opens the list of the datasets that have no pin | — |
| Rows per page | **N (fit)** (as many as fit the height of the window, 10 to 100), or 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 rows | **N (fit)** |

On a large screen, the lists keep a readable width: the organizations and datasets show in two columns of
cards from 2560 px, and text pages (Markdown, the editor) have a line length that is easy to read. On a phone, the tables of Hub (datasets, tasks, members) show each row as a card, with the name of each value
before it.

Hub remembers the view, the sort and the rows per page, and saves them to your account. Set the
defaults in [Preferences](/hub-guide/account/preferences.mdx) on the **Lists** tab.

## What the values mean

- **Modified**: the last time the index of the dataset changed. Hub counts added, removed, moved
  or renamed files, and changes to metadata or visibility. Builds do not count. The value can be a
  short time late.
- The **Modified** time of an organization is the newest of its datasets. Hub shows it, and the number
  of datasets, only for organizations that you own or are a member of.
- A dataset whose files spread over more than 1 degree, for example a survey of several places, has its pin at its
  main cluster, the place with most of its files, and not at the middle of the box, which can be in the sea. The map keeps every pin clear of
  its toolbar and of the controls at the bottom.
- A dataset gets a pin when at least one of its files has coordinates: an orthophoto, an elevation
  model, a point cloud, a vector file or a geotagged image. You cannot place a dataset by hand.
- On the datasets page, a tag shows your role in the organization: **Viewer**, **Editor**, **Editor+**
  or **Admin**. See [Organization members & roles](/hub-guide/datasets/members.mdx).

## Limits & requirements

- The **Organizations** page needs a signed-in account.
- On narrow screens, Hub hides some columns of the datasets list.
