## When to use it

Create an organization for a team or a client, and add the people who work on its datasets.
Each member gets a permission level. The permission level applies to all the datasets of the
organization.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. To create an organization, click **Organizations** in the header and then **Create Organization**.
   Type a **Name**, check the **URL (slug)** that Hub suggests from it (you can edit it) and type an optional
   **Description**. Both fields show a counter against their limit (256 characters for the name, 2000 for the
   description). Select **Public** for a public organization, and click **Create**. The window checks the
   fields on **Create** and shows a refusal at its field (a taken slug, a name without a Latin letter or a
   digit when the slug has none); it stays open with what you typed. Anyone, signed in or not, can see a public organization and its public
   datasets. Only members can change it.

   <Screenshot id="datasets/members--create-organization" />

2. To change the name, description or **Public** later, click the pencil button (**Edit organization**)
   on the card of the organization, or next to its name on its datasets page.
3. To manage the members, open the datasets page of the organization and click **Members**.
   On the **Organizations** page, the people button (**Manage members**) on the card does the same.
   The window lists the members by user name. A long list scrolls inside the window, and **Close** stays
   at the bottom.
4. Under **Add Member**, type the exact **User name** and select the **Permissions**. Click **Add**,
   or press Enter. If the server refuses the name (no such user, or already a member), the window
   shows its message at the field, for example **No user named x**.

   <Screenshot id="datasets/members--add" />

5. To change the level of a member, select a new level in the **Permissions** column. Hub saves it at
   once.
6. To remove a member, click **Remove** and confirm. On a phone, each member is a card with **Remove** in
   it: you do not scroll sideways.

</Steps>

## Permission levels

| Permissions | Role tag | What the member can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Read Only** | Viewer | Open and download the datasets of the organization |
| **Read/Write** | Editor | Also create datasets, upload, edit, move and build files (the default for a new member) |
| **Read/Write/Delete** | Editor+ | Also delete files and datasets |
| **Admin** | Admin | Also manage the members and edit the organization (name, description, **Public**) |

The role tag shows on the datasets page of the organization, next to the number of datasets.

The owner of the organization and the administrators of the server always have all the rights on
its datasets.

## Limits & requirements

- The owner and the members with the **Admin** permission manage the members, when the server
  administrator turns member management on (`EnableOrganizationMemberManagement`, off by default). When it is off,
  nobody can manage the members, server administrators included, and the **Members** and **Manage members** buttons do not show.
- Hub does not list the users of the server. Ask the person for their exact user name.
- The slug is suggested from the organization name, and you can edit it when you create the organization. It cannot change after creation, so editing the name later does not change the address.
- **Public** applies to the organization only. Each dataset keeps its own
  [visibility](/hub-guide/sharing/visibility.mdx).
- The server administrator can make organizations read-only in Hub. Then the create, edit and delete
  buttons do not show.
