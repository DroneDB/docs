## When to use it

Delete a dataset when you do not need its files any more, for example to free storage. Delete an
organization to remove it and all of its datasets. You cannot undo a delete.

To delete only some files of a dataset, see [Folders, rename & delete](/hub-guide/files/folders.mdx).

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open the datasets page of the organization.
2. Click the red trash button (**Delete dataset**) in the **Actions** column of the dataset. You can
   also right-click the row and click **Delete**.
3. Read the warning and click **Remove**.
4. To delete an organization, open **My Organizations** and click the red trash button
   (**Delete organization**) on its card. Read the warning and click **Remove**.

   <Screenshot id="datasets/delete--organization-confirm" />

</Steps>

## What happens

- The dataset leaves the list at once. The server then deletes its folder in the background: the
  files, the builds and the thumbnails. It also stops the builds that run for the dataset.
- When you delete an organization, Hub deletes all of its datasets too.
- Links and embeds of a deleted dataset stop working.

## Limits & requirements

- You need the delete permission: you own the organization, you are a server administrator, or you
  are a member with **Read/Write/Delete** or **Admin**. See [Organization members & roles](/hub-guide/datasets/members.mdx).
- The shared organization `public` has no delete button.
- The server administrator can turn off dataset creation. Then the delete buttons of datasets do
  not show either.
- Copy the files that you want to keep before you delete. See [Download files & ZIP export](/hub-guide/files/download.mdx).
