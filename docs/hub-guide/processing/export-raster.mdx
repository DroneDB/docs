## When to use it

You styled a raster, for example an NDVI map with a colormap, a false-colour band combination or a
thermal mosaic with Ironbow, and you want that picture as a file for a report or a GIS program. Export
writes it as a GeoTIFF and your browser downloads it. The file is a **styled image**, not the values:
for the temperatures or elevations themselves, download the original file or use the
[CLI](/developers/cli/cookbook.mdx).

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open the raster on the map.

2. Click **Plant Health** (a raster with two bands or more) or **Raster Analysis** (a raster with one
   band: a DEM, a thermal mosaic) in the layer controls.

3. Set the view: a **Preset**, a **Formula**, the **Bands (R,G,B)**, the **Colormap**, the **Stretch** or the
   **Rescale** range. See [Plant health](/hub-guide/analyse/plant-health.mdx) and
   [Colormaps, bands & histogram](/hub-guide/analyse/colormaps.mdx) for each control.

   <Screenshot id="processing/export-raster--formula" />

4. In **Export**, click **Export** for the whole raster, or **Visible extent** for the part that the map
   shows now. The panel shows the task: **Queued** while it waits, then **Exporting** with the percent
   and a **Cancel** button.

5. When the file is ready, the toast **Export ready:** names it and your browser downloads it:
   `<name>_export.tif`, or `<name>_extent_export.tif` for the visible extent.

</Steps>

## What the file holds

The name of the **Export** button follows your settings, and the panel says the same under it. With a
formula, or with a colormap on a single band (a DEM, or one band that you chose), it is **Export styled
image (RGBA GeoTIFF, in the CRS of the file)**, and the text under it ends "the picture of the style, not
the values". Otherwise it is **Export scaled bands (8-bit GeoTIFF, in the CRS of the file)**, and the text ends
"the bands scaled to 8 bits, not the values". Either way the file is 8-bit, so it never holds the values.

| Your settings | The exported GeoTIFF |
|---|---|
| A **Formula** (an index, or the unit of a thermal raster) | An 8-bit picture (red, green, blue and transparency) painted with the **Colormap** and the **Rescale** range. It does not hold the index or the temperature values. |
| A **Colormap** on a single band, no formula (since DroneDB 2.3.1): a raster with one band, such as a DEM, or one band that you selected | The same kind of picture: 8-bit red, green, blue and transparency, painted with the **Colormap** over the **Rescale** range, or over the default stretch of the raster (its lowest to its highest value) when there is none. Pixels with no value are transparent. This is what **Raster Analysis** exports for an elevation model. |
| **Bands (R,G,B)**, no formula | The bands you selected, as 8-bit values scaled to the **Rescale** range, with no colormap (several bands have no colormap). |

A server older than DroneDB 2.3.1 does not paint a single band without a formula: it writes one 8-bit grey band, and
the name of the button says a styled image all the same.

The file has the coordinate system, the pixel size and the position of the raster, not Web Mercator. **Visible extent**
is the part of the raster under the map, at the full resolution of the raster, so it is not bigger than
the raster: use it to take a large raster in parts.

The export runs as a task on the server: **Export raster (GeoTIFF)** in the [Tasks tab](/hub-guide/processing/tasks.mdx). There
you can download the result again until it expires, and read how long it waited in the queue (**waited 50s**) next to its duration.

## Limits & requirements

- Read access is enough: you can export from any public dataset.
- A raster of one band has the export in **Raster Analysis**, and a raster of two bands or more has it in **Plant Health**.
- The server limits the size of an export. When the estimated file is too large, **Export** is
  greyed out but you can still reach it with the keyboard, and the panel says why in the text under
  it: **Too large to export: about 9.3 GB > 1 GB limit. Use Visible extent to export it in parts.**
  The estimate is the pixels that have a value × 4 bytes. **Visible extent** gives its own estimate
  (**Too large to export the visible extent: … Zoom in to export less.**), and says **Checking size…** while it
  reads the size of a new view.
- **Visible extent** needs a server with DroneDB 2.3 or later. On an older one the button is greyed out
  with **Needs a newer server (libddb 2.3)** and **Export** exports the whole raster.
- One export runs at a time in a panel: both buttons wait until it ends or you click **Cancel**.
