## When to use it

Use the map to see the files of a dataset that have a position: orthophotos, elevation models, photos, videos, panoramas, vector files and georeferenced splats. There are two maps:

- The **Map** tab of a dataset shows every file with a position in the folder on show.
- The map of one file opens in a new browser tab when you double-click a georaster or a vector file, or when you choose **Open Map** in its context menu.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open a dataset and click the **Map** tab. Hub draws the files of the folder on show.

   Geotagged photos are blue dots. The line between them is the flight path, from the start flag to the finish flag.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/map--flight-shots" />

2. Hold the pointer on a photo to see its name. Click it to open its popup: a preview, **Open in new tab**, **Download** and **Copy coordinates**. Hub also draws the ground footprint of the photo.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/map--photo-popup" />

3. Click **Show direction and speed indicators** (top center) to draw each photo that records its camera direction as an arrow. The tooltip then also shows the speed.

4. Drag the opacity slider at the bottom to see the basemap through a raster. On a phone or in a small embed the layer panel starts folded. Long layer names are shortened in the middle, so two dates of one orthophoto can still be told apart. With more than one raster, a layer panel sets the opacity, the order and the eye (hide or show) of each one (see [Compare layers](/hub-guide/analyse/compare-layers.mdx)). On the map of one file, **Add layer** puts another georaster of the dataset on top of it, and **Save** in the bar above the map keeps the view (see [Map views & saved views](/hub-guide/viewing/map-views.mdx)).

5. Click the gear button (bottom left) to open **Map Settings**. Choose a **Basemap** and the **Measurement Units**.

6. To use your own basemap, choose **Custom** as the basemap. Set the **Source type** (**XYZ tiles** or **WMS**) and the **URL**. For WMS, click **Fetch layers**, then choose the **Layer** and the **Tile size**. Click **Apply**. Hub loads one tile to check the source, then saves it to your account.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/map--custom-basemap" />

7. Hold the pointer on a vector feature to see its name. Double-click the feature to open **Feature Properties**, a table of its attributes. GeoJSON, KML/KMZ, GeoPackage, Shapefile, GPX tracks and DXF/DWG drawings with a coordinate system all show this way.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/map--vector-features" />

</Steps>

## Options

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Basemap** | **Satellite**, **Hybrid**, **OpenStreetMap** or **Custom** (an XYZ or WMS source) | Satellite |
| **Measurement Units** | **Metric** or **Imperial**, for measurements and speeds | Metric |
| **Show drone flight path** | The path through the timed photos, with start and finish flags | On |
| **Show direction and speed indicators** | Photos as arrows; speed in the tooltip | Off |
| **Reset View** (or the `H` key) | Zooms back to the first view of the files | — |
| **Fullscreen** | The map fills the screen | — |
| **Rectangle Selection**, **Polygon Selection** (top left, **Map** tab only) | Select the files inside a shape in the file list. `Ctrl`+click selects one file | — |

The basemap, the custom basemap, the flight path and the direction indicators are [preferences](/hub-guide/account/preferences.mdx): they follow your account.

Other items on the map:

- **Panoramas** are a separate marker. Click one to open the [panorama viewer](/hub-guide/viewing/panoramas.mdx).
- **Geotagged videos** open a popup with **Play video**. See [Videos](/hub-guide/viewing/videos.mdx).
- **Georeferenced splats** show as a point, then as their ground footprint after the build. Double-click one to open it. See [Georeferenced splats](/hub-guide/viewing/splats-georeferenced.mdx).

## Limits & requirements

- A file shows on the map only when it has a position. A drawing without a coordinate system opens in the [3D viewer](/hub-guide/viewing/3d-viewer.mdx) instead.
- Rasters and vector files show after their build is done. See [Builds](/hub-guide/processing/builds.mdx).
