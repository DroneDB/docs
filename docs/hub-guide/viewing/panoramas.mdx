## When to use it

Use the panorama viewer to look around a 360° photo as if you stand where it was taken. Hub finds panoramas by themselves: an image that is at least twice as wide as it is high is a panorama. You upload it like any other photo.

<LiveEmbed org="odm" ds="langley" path="langley.png" view="panorama" poster="viewing/panoramas--embed" />

## Steps

<Steps>

1. In the file list, double-click a panorama, or right-click it and choose **Open Panorama**. The viewer opens in a new browser tab.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/panoramas--viewer" />

2. Drag to look around. Turn the mouse wheel, or pinch on a touch screen, to zoom.

3. Use the bar at the bottom of the viewer:
   - **Automatic rotation** turns the view slowly.
   - **Zoom out**, the zoom slider and **Zoom in** change the zoom.
   - **Move** turns the view up, down, left or right.
   - **Fullscreen** fills the screen.

4. If the panorama has a GPS position, open the **Map** tab of the dataset. The panorama is a marker of its own. Click the marker to open the panorama.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/panoramas--map-marker" />

</Steps>

## Limits & requirements

- The viewer shows equirectangular panoramas: the usual format of 360° cameras and of stitching software.
- A panorama with GPS tags in its file (a geo-panorama) is also on the map. A panorama without them is only in the file list.
- The viewer uses the orientation that the camera wrote in the file (heading, pitch and roll), when there is one.
- To download the original image, use **Download** in the file list. See [Download files](../files/download.mdx).
