## When to use it

Hub plays the videos of a dataset in the browser, so you can check a flight or an inspection clip without a download. A video with a GPS position in its file is a **geo-video**: it also shows on the map of the dataset.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. In the file list, double-click a video. It plays in a player over the page.

2. Use the controls of the player to pause, seek, change the volume or go full screen. Click **Open in new tab** to play the video in its own browser tab.

3. Click **Close**, press `Escape` or click beside the video to close the player.

4. For a geo-video, open the **Map** tab of the dataset. The video is a marker, like a photo. Click the marker to see its popup: the name, the resolution, the date, **Download**, the coordinates and **Play video**.

   <Screenshot id="viewing/videos--map-popup" />

5. Click **Play video** in the popup, or double-click the marker, to play the video.

</Steps>

## Limits & requirements

- The browser plays the video. Most browsers play MP4 (H.264) and WEBM. AVI, MKV and some MOV files may not play. The player then says "Could not play video". Click **Download** in the file list and play the file on your computer.
- A geo-video shows as one marker, at the position in its file. Hub does not draw the track of the video.
- Hub makes no thumbnail of a video.
