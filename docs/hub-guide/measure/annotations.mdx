## When to use it

Use a point annotation to mark a place on the map: a defect, a ground control point, a tree to
check. The annotation has a colour and a description, and it shows the coordinates of the point.
It is a measurement, so you save, list and export it with the other
[measurements on the map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx).

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open an orthophoto on the map, or open the **Map** tab of a dataset.

2. Click **Point Annotation** in the measure toolbar. Click the point on the map.

3. In the **Point Annotation** dialog, select a **Color** and type a **Description**. Click
   **Save**. **Discard** removes the point.

   <Screenshot id="measure/annotations--dialog" />

4. The label of the point shows its coordinates and the description. To add more points, click
   on the map again. Press `Esc` to stop.

5. Click **Save Measurements** in the toolbar to save the annotations in the dataset.

</Steps>

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Color** | The colour of the point and of its label. |
| **Description** | Free text that shows in the label. |
| Name | Double-click the point to open its properties: a name, which shows as the title of the label, and a group. |

The label of a saved point has three buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit annotation** | Opens the **Point Annotation** dialog again. |
| **Copy coordinates** | Copies `latitude, longitude` in decimal degrees. |
| **Delete point** | Removes the point from the map. Save to remove it from the dataset. |

The coordinates are WGS84, in degrees, minutes and seconds and in decimal degrees (6 decimals).

## Limits & requirements

- Anyone who can open the map can add points. Only users with write access to the dataset can
  save them.
- Point annotations are saved in the same file as the other measurements of the map. See
  [Measurements on the map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx#limits--requirements).
- The [point cloud viewer](/hub-guide/measure/3d.mdx) has its own annotations, in 3D.
