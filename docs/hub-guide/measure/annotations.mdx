## When to use it

Use a point annotation to mark a place on the map: a defect, a ground control point, a tree to
check. The annotation has a colour and a description, and it shows the coordinates of the point.
It is a measurement, so you save, list and export it with the other
[measurements on the map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx).

Every annotation, also a length or an area, has a short **reference** such as `12`. You can add
inspection fields (category, severity, status, who must act and by when) and photos. A
[report](/hub-guide/measure/reports.mdx) uses all of them.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open an orthophoto on the map, or open the **Map** tab of a dataset.

2. Click **Point Annotation** in the measure toolbar. Click the point on the map.

3. In the **Point Annotation** dialog, select a **Color** and type a **Description**. Click
   **Save**. **Discard** removes the point.

   <Screenshot id="measure/annotations--dialog" />

4. The label of the point shows its coordinates and the description. To add more points, click
   on the map again. Press `Esc` to stop.

5. To fill the inspection fields and to add photos, double-click the point. The window has two
   tabs: **Details** and **Photos**. See [Inspection fields](#inspection-fields) and
   [Photos](#photos).

   <Screenshot id="measure/annotations--fields" />

6. Click **Save Measurements** in the toolbar to save the annotations in the dataset.

</Steps>

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Color** | The colour of the point and of its label. |
| **Description** | Free text that shows in the label. |
| Name | Double-click the point to open its properties: a name, which shows as the title of the label, and a group. |

The label of a saved point has three buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit annotation** | Opens the **Point Annotation** dialog again. |
| **Copy coordinates** | Copies `latitude, longitude` in decimal degrees. |
| **Delete point** | Asks first, then removes the point from the map. Save to remove it from the dataset. The **Delete** button of the **Point Annotation** dialog asks the same question. |

The coordinates are WGS84, in degrees, minutes and seconds and in decimal degrees (6 decimals).

### References

Hub gives each new annotation the next number as its **Reference**: `1`, `2`, `3`. The map draws it
next to the annotation. Click **Show References** in the second row of the measure toolbar to hide
it or to show it again. It is on at the start.

A reference does not change when you add, delete or reorder other annotations. A number is not used
again, also after you delete its annotation. You can type another reference: 1 to 8 letters, digits
or dashes. Hub refuses one that another annotation of the file has: **Reference 12 is already
used**. If another person took your number before you saved, Hub gives you the next one and tells
you: **Reference 12 was taken; the item is now 13**.

The map and the [point cloud viewer](/hub-guide/measure/3d.mdx) share one file, so they share the
numbers. Annotations that an older version saved get their reference the first time Hub saves the
file, or when you save a report that reads it.

### Inspection fields

The **Inspection** group of the **Details** tab has these fields. They are the same for 2D and 3D
annotations.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Reference** | See [References](#references). Empty, it says **Assigned when saved**. |
| **Category** | What the annotation is: for example **Defect** or **Safety**. |
| **Severity** | How bad it is. It shows when the organization has severities. |
| **Status** | Where it is in the work: for example **Open** or **Resolved**. |
| **Assignee** | A name, free text, up to 100 characters. |
| **Due date** | A date. A report counts an annotation as overdue when its status is not closed and its due date is past. |
| **Include in reports** | On at the start. Switch it off to leave the annotation out of every report. |

A new annotation has no category, severity or status until you choose one. The choices come from
the [annotation schema of the organization](/hub-guide/measure/report-settings.mdx). A value that the
schema no longer has shows as **Unknown (id)**: you can keep it. The list of measurements
shows the reference, the category and the status of each row, and filters by category and status.

### Photos

The **Photos** tab of the window shows the photos of the annotation, with their number in the tab
name. An annotation has up to 12 photos. Each has a caption of up to 200 characters.

<Screenshot id="measure/annotations--photos" />

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Remove** | Takes the photo off the annotation. The file stays in the dataset. |
| **Upload** | Adds JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF files from your computer. Hub writes each file in the dataset, in an `annotations` folder next to the measurement file, as `<annotation id>-<number>.<extension>`. |
| **Pick from the dataset** | Searches the images of the dataset by name and adds one. |
| **Add** | Pins a photo from **Photos that cover this annotation**. |

**Photos that cover this annotation** lists the images of the dataset that show the place, best
first: **Best match**, **Match 2**, and so on, up to 20. Hub reads them from the position and the
camera data of drone photos. When it knows where the place is in a photo, a marker with the name
**Approximate location** shows there. Treat it as a guide, not as a measurement.

If no photo covers the place, the tab says **No covering photos found**. If the search fails, it
shows the error and **Retry**. An annotation without a position on the earth cannot be searched:
pick or upload a photo. Users without write access see the photos and the captions, and cannot
change them.

## Limits & requirements

- Anyone who can open the map can add points. Only users with write access to the dataset can
  save them.
- Point annotations are saved in the same file as the other measurements of the map. See
  [Measurements on the map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx#limits--requirements).
- The [point cloud viewer](/hub-guide/measure/3d.mdx) has its own annotations, in 3D.
- Deleting an annotation does not delete its uploaded photos. Delete them in the file browser.
- Hub keeps the properties of an annotation that it does not know when it saves the file, so
  the additions of another program stay.
