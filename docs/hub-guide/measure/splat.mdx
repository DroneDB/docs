## When to use it

Measure a splat to get the size of an object in the scene: the length of a wall, the height of a
door, the area of a floor. Add pins to explain what a visitor sees: a pin has a title, a text in
Markdown, a link and a picture. Measurements, pins and the scale are saved with the splat, next to
its camera views.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open a Gaussian splat. The measure toolbar is at the top left.

2. Click **Measure a distance**. Click the points of the line. Press `Enter` or right-click to
   finish. Press `Escape` to cancel. On a screen without a right click, use **Done** and
   **Cancel** under the toolbar.

   <Screenshot id="measure/splat--distance" />

3. Click **Measure a height** and click two points. The height is the difference between them
   along the up direction of the scene.

4. If the values are in the units of the file, set the scale. Click **Set the scale**. Click the
   two ends of something whose length you know. In **Set the scale**, type its **Real length
   (metres)** and click **Set scale**. All values are then in metres.

   <Screenshot id="measure/splat--set-scale" />

5. To add a pin, click **Pins**, then **Add a pin**. Click where the pin goes in the scene. Give
   it a name, a **Text** in Markdown, a **Link** and an **Image**.

   <Screenshot id="measure/splat--pins" />

6. Click **Save** in the **Pins** panel. This saves the pins, the measurements, the scale and the
   camera views.

</Steps>

## Options

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Measure a point** | Shows the position of a point: from the origin of the file, in file units or in metres. |
| **Measure a distance** | A line through any number of points. |
| **Measure a height** | The difference between two points along the up direction of the scene. |
| **Measure an area** | A polygon of three or more points. |
| **Clear measurements** | Removes all measurements. |
| **Set the scale** | Sets how many metres a unit of the file is, from two points and a real length. Setting it again replaces it. |
| Coordinates button | On a [georeferenced splat](../viewing/splats-georeferenced.mdx) only: shows a point in the coordinate system of the splat or in WGS84. |

A pin has these fields:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Name | The title of the pin card. |
| **Text** | Markdown text in the card. |
| **Link** | A web address. It opens in a new tab. |
| **Image** | The path of a picture in the same dataset, for example `photos/gate.jpg`. |

In the **Pins** panel you can show a pin, save the current view with a pin, reorder the pins and
remove them. Clicking a pin in the scene opens its card and flies to its saved view. The tour of
the splat visits the pins that have a view, in the order of the list.

## Limits & requirements

- Anyone who can open the splat sees its saved pins and measurements, and can measure.
  What a user without write access measures is not saved.
- Only users with write access can add and edit pins, set the scale and save.
- Until the scene has a scale, values are in the units of the file. A note under the toolbar
  says so.
- A georeferenced splat is in metres already: **Set the scale** does not show.
- If the saved views of the splat cannot be read, the viewer warns you. Saving then asks before
  it replaces them.
