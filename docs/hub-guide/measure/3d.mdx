## When to use it

Measure in 3D when the height matters: the height of a building, the slope of a roof, the
distance between two points at different heights. The point cloud viewer has the most tools and
saves its measurements in the dataset. The 3D viewer measures points, distances and areas on
any data it shows, for the session only.

## Steps

### Point cloud viewer

<Steps>

1. Open a point cloud. Saved measurements of the point cloud show automatically.

2. Click a measuring tool in the toolbar at the top left of the view: **Point**, **Distance**, **Height** or **Area**.
   The other tools (angle, circle, volume, height profile) are in the **Tools** section of the side menu (the
   menu button of the viewer).

   <Screenshot id="measure/3d--pointcloud-tools" />

3. Click the points on the point cloud. Double-click, right-click or press `Enter` to finish. Press `Esc` to
   cancel: the shape is dropped. A right-drag pans the view and does not finish the shape.

4. Double-click a measurement to give it a name and a description, or to change its colours.
   The window has the same **Inspection** fields and **Photos** tab as the map: reference,
   category, severity, status, assignee, due date and photos. See
   [Point annotations](/hub-guide/measure/annotations.mdx). Click **Save**.

   The label of the measurement shows its name, its category and severity (with the colour of
   the severity) and the first line of its description. A point with a label shows it in place
   of its coordinates. A point with no name, category, severity or description shows its
   coordinates.

5. To give the measurement a picture for a [report](/hub-guide/measure/reports.mdx), click
   **Capture for report** in the same window. Hub frames the measurement and keeps one image of
   the 3D view, with its label. The window opens the **Photos** tab, which shows the picture
   under **3D view for reports**. The tab counts the picture with the photos. A new capture
   replaces the old one. The picture is the measurement's at once, also when you click
   **Cancel**, and the measurement file keeps it when you save the measurements.

   <Screenshot id="measure/3d--capture" />

6. Click **Save** in the toolbar to save the measurements in the dataset.

   <Screenshot id="measure/3d--pointcloud-saved" />

</Steps>

### 3D viewer

<Steps>

1. Right-click a point cloud, a georaster, a vector file, a 3D model or a 3D Tiles file. Select
   **Experimental**, then **Unified Viewer**.

2. Click **Measure point**, **Measure distance** or **Measure area** at the top left. Click on
   the scene. Double-click or right-click to finish a shape. Press `Esc` or click **Cancel** to drop it.

   <Screenshot id="measure/3d--unified-area" />

3. Click **Clear measurements** to remove them.

</Steps>

## Options

### Point cloud viewer

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Point, distance, height, circle, angle, area measurement | The measurements that **Save** saves. |
| Volume measurement, height profile | Work in the viewer. They are not saved. |
| **Annotation** | A labelled 3D point with a name and a description. Double-click it to edit it, to copy its position, to set its inspection fields and photos, or to capture it. It is saved with the measurements. |
| **Capture for report** | In the window of a measurement or an annotation, for users with write access. Writes an image of the 3D view, framed on the item, to the dataset. |
| **Undo** / **Redo** | Undo or redo adding or deleting a measurement (`Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Y`). |
| **Delete measurement tool** | Click a measurement to delete it. |
| **Units** | In the bar at the bottom left. **Metric (m)** or **Imperial (ft)**. The same setting as the **Measurement Units** of the map and the **Units** tab of the [preferences](/docs/hub-guide/account/preferences). |
| **Save** | Makes the saved file the same as the viewer. |
| **Delete saved measurements** | In the menu beside **Save** (the three dots). Deletes the saved 3D measurements from the dataset. |

### 3D viewer

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Measure point** | The position of a point. |
| **Measure distance** | A line through two or more points, with the length of each segment. |
| **Measure area** | A polygon and its area. |
| **Clear measurements** | Removes all measurements. |

The 3D viewer writes lengths and areas in the units of your
[preferences](/hub-guide/account/preferences.mdx), on the same scale as the
[map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx#units): areas in m², ha and km², or ft² and acres; imperial lengths in
feet. Very small values are in cm, mm, cm², mm², inches or in². When you change the units, the labels
change at once.

## Limits & requirements

- **Save** shows only for users with write access to the dataset.
  **Delete saved measurements** shows only for users who can delete in the dataset.
- The lines and the labels of the measurements grow with the size of the view, from 12 to 18 px for the labels and 3 px for the lines, so they stay readable on a 4K screen.
- The point cloud viewer saves next to the point cloud, with `_measurements.geojson` in place of
  the extension. The [map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx) uses the same file only when the point cloud is the one
  file on the map; with a folder, several files or layers on the map, it saves in the `measurements.geojson` of the folder.
  Each view saves and deletes only its own measurements; the other view's measurements and groups stay
  in the file.
- The file is in format 3.0. A file in an older format opens as it is and is upgraded the next
  time you save. Opening a file never changes it. A Hub page that you opened before Hub was
  updated cannot save a 3.0 file: it says that the file is of a newer format. Reload the page, then
  save again.
- A capture is a WebP file in the `annotations` folder next to the measurement file:
  `<id>-3d.webp`. Deleting the measurement does not delete it. A measurement with no capture shows
  **No 3D snapshot** in a report.
- The photos of a 3D measurement are searched from its position. A point cloud without a
  coordinate system has no position on the earth, so the **Photos** tab offers only **Upload** and
  **Pick from the dataset**. A report shows **Not georeferenced** for it.
- The saved coordinates are in the coordinate system of the point cloud, converted to WGS84:
  longitude and latitude, with the height in the units of the point cloud. A GIS program and the
  map read the right place. A point cloud without a coordinate system saves its local coordinates,
  with no `crs`.
- The 3D viewer does not save its measurements.
- The 3D viewer is experimental.
