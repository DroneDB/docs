## When to use it

Set the look of the [reports](/hub-guide/measure/reports.mdx) and the words of the
[inspection fields](/hub-guide/measure/annotations.mdx#inspection-fields) once for the whole
organization. Two members see the same templates and the same categories, severities and statuses.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Click **Organizations** in the header. On the card of the organization, click **Report
   settings** (the button with a page icon). Every member sees this button.

2. In the **Templates** tab, select a template, or click **Add template**. Type a **Name**. Set the
   **Primary colour** and the **Accent colour**, the **Paper**, the **Orientation**, the **Summary
   charts**, the **Header text**, the **Footer text** and the **Disclaimer**. The **Preview** shows the cover of a report with
   your values: the logo and the header text, the title on the primary colour, the four headline
   figures, a sample item, the disclaimer and the footer.

   <Screenshot id="measure/report-settings--preview" />

3. Under **Sections of a new report**, tick the parts that a new report has.

4. Under **Logo**, select **Organization logo** or **Custom logo**. **Organization logo** shows the
   logo of the organization, which you set with **Edit organization** (see
   [Organizations, members & roles](/hub-guide/datasets/members.mdx)). For a logo of this template
   only, select **Custom logo**, click **Save** if the template is new, then click **Upload logo** and
   select an image or SVG of up to 20 MB. Hub prepares it in your browser: a larger image is shrunk to
   600 pixels on its longest side and saved as WebP, and an SVG stays an SVG. A message says what was
   saved, for example **Saved as WebP, 600 × 338, 61 KB**. The logo is stored at once, without
   **Save**. To go back to the logo of the organization, select **Organization logo** and confirm:
   Hub removes the custom logo.

5. In the **Annotation schema** tab, edit the **Categories**, the **Severities** and the
   **Statuses**. Click **Add category**, **Add severity** or **Add status**, type a name, and pick a
   colour. Tick **Closed** for a status that ends the work. Use the arrows to change the order.

   <Screenshot id="measure/report-settings--schema" />

6. Click **Save**. The window stays open.

</Steps>

## Options

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name in the **Template** list of the report editor. 1 to 60 characters. |
| **Primary colour** | The band of the cover, the bar along the top of each page, the section titles, the headers of the tables, and the card of an item that has no category colour. The text on it is white or dark, whichever is easier to read. |
| **Accent colour** | The stripe under the band of the cover, the bar under each section title, the bar along the bottom of each page, the page number, the totals of the summary and the links in the notes. |
| **Paper**, **Orientation** | **A4** or **Letter**; **Portrait** or **Landscape**. The PDF uses them. |
| **Summary charts** | How the **Breakdown** of the summary shows the number of items by type, category, severity and status, on the live page and in the PDF. **Bars** (the default): one bar for each value, as long as its count is of the largest count of its group. **Donuts**: one ring for each group, with a slice for each value and the number of items in the middle, and a legend with the count and the percentage of each value. In a donut, each type has a shade of the accent colour. |
| **Header text**, **Footer text** | Up to 200 characters each. The header text goes on every page of the PDF but the cover, with the logo and the title of the report. The footer text goes on every page, with the page number. |
| **Disclaimer** | Markdown, up to 5000 characters. It closes every report. |
| **Sections of a new report** | A new report copies them. Reports that exist keep their sections. |
| **Logo** | **Organization logo** (the default) or **Custom logo**: any image or SVG of up to 20 MB, which Hub stores as at most 512 KB. **Replace logo** changes a custom logo. |

An organization that stored nothing has the template **Standard**, and this schema:

| List | Values |
|---|---|
| **Categories** | General, Defect, Observation, Safety, Stockpile |
| **Severities** | Low, Medium, High, Critical |
| **Statuses** | Open, In progress, Resolved (closed) |

## Limits & requirements

- Only an administrator of the organization changes the settings: the owner, a member with the
  **Admin** permission, or a Registry administrator. Other members see the settings and a note.
  Readers of a dataset get the templates and the schema of its organization through the dataset.
- An organization has up to 20 templates, 50 categories, 10 severities and 10 statuses. It keeps at
  least one template, one category and one status.
- The **ID** of a template or of a value is made from its name and does not change after you save.
  A rename changes only the name that shows. Annotations keep their values.
- A template that you delete goes when you save. The reports that use it use the first template,
  and say so.
- A report shows the custom logo of its template. Without one, it shows the logo of the
  organization, and without that, the logo of the site, if the site has one. A reader of a public
  dataset of a private organization also sees the logo of the organization on its reports.
- If someone else saved the settings while you edited, Hub says **The settings changed**. Click
  **Reload** to see their version. Your changes are lost.
