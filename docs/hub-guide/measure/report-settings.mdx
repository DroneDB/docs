## When to use it

Set the look of the [reports](/hub-guide/measure/reports.mdx) and the words of the
[inspection fields](/hub-guide/measure/annotations.mdx#inspection-fields) once for the whole
organization. Two members see the same templates and the same categories, severities and statuses.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Click **Organizations** in the header. On the card of the organization, click **Report
   settings** (the button with a page icon). Every member sees this button.

2. In the **Templates** tab, select a template, or click **Add template**. Type a **Name**. Set the
   **Primary colour** and the **Accent colour**, the **Paper**, the **Orientation**, the **Header
   text**, the **Footer text** and the **Disclaimer**. The **Preview** shows a page with your values.

   <Screenshot id="measure/report-settings--preview" />

3. Under **Sections of a new report**, tick the parts that a new report has.

4. To add a logo, click **Save**, then **Upload logo**, and select the file. A logo is stored at
   once, without **Save**.

5. In the **Annotation schema** tab, edit the **Categories**, the **Severities** and the
   **Statuses**. Click **Add category**, **Add severity** or **Add status**, type a name, and pick a
   colour. Tick **Closed** for a status that ends the work. Use the arrows to change the order.

   <Screenshot id="measure/report-settings--schema" />

6. Click **Save**. The window stays open.

</Steps>

## Options

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name in the **Template** list of the report editor. 1 to 60 characters. |
| **Primary colour** | The colour of the titles and of the headings of the PDF. |
| **Accent colour** | The colour of the links in the notes of the PDF. |
| **Paper**, **Orientation** | **A4** or **Letter**; **Portrait** or **Landscape**. The PDF uses them. |
| **Header text**, **Footer text** | Up to 200 characters each. They go on every page of the PDF, with the logo and the page number. |
| **Disclaimer** | Markdown, up to 5000 characters. It closes every report. |
| **Sections of a new report** | A new report copies them. Reports that exist keep their sections. |
| **Logo** | PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG, up to 512 KB. **Replace logo** and **Remove logo** change it. |

An organization that stored nothing has the template **Standard**, and this schema:

| List | Values |
|---|---|
| **Categories** | General, Defect, Observation, Safety, Stockpile |
| **Severities** | Low, Medium, High, Critical |
| **Statuses** | Open, In progress, Resolved (closed) |

## Limits & requirements

- Only an administrator of the organization changes the settings: the owner, a member with the
  **Admin** permission, or a Registry administrator. Other members see the settings and a note.
  Readers of a dataset get the templates and the schema of its organization through the dataset.
- An organization has up to 20 templates, 50 categories, 10 severities and 10 statuses. It keeps at
  least one template, one category and one status.
- The **ID** of a template or of a value is made from its name and does not change after you save.
  A rename changes only the name that shows. Annotations keep their values.
- A template that you delete goes when you save. The reports that use it use the first template,
  and say so.
- Without a logo, a report shows the logo of the site, if the site has one.
- If someone else saved the settings while you edited, Hub says **The settings changed**. Click
  **Reload** to see their version. Your changes are lost.
