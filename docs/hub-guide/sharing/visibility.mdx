## When to use it

Every dataset has a visibility. It decides who can open the dataset without being a member of its
organization. A new dataset is **Private**. Make it **Unlisted** to share it by link, or **Public** to
publish it.

## The three levels

| Visibility | Who can see and download the data | Listed for others |
|---|---|---|
| **Private** | Only you, the members of your organization and the administrators | No |
| **Unlisted** | Anybody who has the link | No |
| **Public** | Anybody | Yes: in the dataset list of a public organization and in the [STAC catalog](./stac.mdx) |

Members of the organization can always open its datasets. What they can change depends on their
[role](../datasets/members.mdx).

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open the dataset.

2. Click **Settings** (the wrench button in the header). The **Dataset Settings** window opens.

3. Under **Sharing**, select the **Visibility**: **Public**, **Unlisted** or **Private**. The line under the
   list says what the choice means.

4. Click **Close**. The change is saved at once: there is no Save button.

</Steps>

You can also choose the visibility when you [create a dataset](../datasets/create.mdx), and for the new
dataset that [photogrammetry](../processing/photogrammetry.mdx) can make.

## From the Share dialog

When you share a file of a private dataset with a link, an embed or an OGC service, the
[Share dialog](./links-embeds.mdx) shows **Allow access to anyone with the link**. This button changes the
visibility of the **whole dataset** to **Unlisted**. To undo it, set the visibility back to **Private** in the
settings.

<Screenshot id="sharing/visibility--allow-access" />

## Limits & requirements

- You need write access to change the visibility. Readers see the setting, but cannot change it.
- A link to an unlisted dataset works for anyone who has it. Treat it like a password: when you set the
  dataset back to **Private**, old links stop working.
- The **License** of the dataset is in the same window. It tells others how they can use public data.
