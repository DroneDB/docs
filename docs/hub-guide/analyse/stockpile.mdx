## When to use it

Use the stockpile tool to measure how much material a pile holds: gravel, sand, soil, coal.
Hub computes the volume from a surface model (DSM) of the pile. Select the material, and Hub
also gives the weight and the cost of the pile.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open the DSM on the map. Click **Stockpile Volume** in the toolbar at the top right.

   <Screenshot id="analyse/stockpile--panel" />

2. Optional: choose the base in **Base Plane Method**. The information button next to it explains
   each method. Until you choose one, a clicked pile uses **Best-fit plane** and a drawn polygon
   uses **Lowest perimeter**. After you choose one, every next pile uses it, and the pile on screen
   is measured again with it.

3. Click **Click map**, then click on the pile. Hub finds the edge of the pile and computes its
   volume against the ground around it, so detection works on a slope too. If Hub does not find
   the right edge, click **Draw** and draw a polygon around the pile. Double-click to finish. On a
   slope, choose **Best-fit plane** for a drawn polygon.

   <Screenshot id="analyse/stockpile--detect" />

   <Screenshot id="analyse/stockpile--draw" />

4. Select a **Material**. The panel shows the **Weight** and the **Est. cost** of the pile.

   <Screenshot id="analyse/stockpile--material-cost" />

5. Type a title and a description under **Identification**. Click **Save Measurements** in the
   measure toolbar to save the pile, or **Export GeoJSON** to download its outline.

</Steps>

## See the pile on an orthophoto

Add the orthophoto as a layer to see the pile as in a photo.

<Steps>

1. Open the DSM on the map. Click **Add layer** in the opacity control at the bottom.

2. Select the orthophoto in any folder. It goes on top of the DSM. You can add up to 3 layers. **Remove layer** takes one off.

   <Screenshot id="analyse/stockpile--ortho-under-dsm" />

</Steps>

With two or more elevation models on the map, **Stockpile Volume** shows an **Elevation model**
selector. At first it uses the model of the measurements, or else the top one. Hub saves a pile with
the model you select. If you select another model with unsaved measurements, Hub asks **Discard the
unsaved measurements of …?**.

An opaque orthophoto on top hides the **Raster Analysis** colours of the DSM below it. Hide it with its
eye in the layer panel, or lower its opacity. The volume does not depend on the other layers.

## Options

| Option | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Base Plane Method** | The surface under the pile. See the table below. | Lowest perimeter for a drawn polygon; Best-fit plane for a detected pile |
| **Detection Sensitivity** | How easily the detection separates the pile from the ground, from 0 to 1. Higher values find lower piles, but can take in nearby ground. | 0.50 |
| **Search Radius (m)** | How far from your click Hub looks for the edge of the pile. Make it larger for a large pile. | 50 |
| **Material (optional)** | The material that gives the weight and the cost. **+ Custom…** lets you type a density and a cost for this pile only. | none |

<Screenshot id="analyse/stockpile--base-methods" />

| Base plane method | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Lowest perimeter** | A flat plane at the lowest point of the outline. Fill is always 0. | A pile on uneven ground. |
| **Average perimeter** | A flat plane at the mean elevation of the outline. | A pile on level ground. |
| **Best-fit plane** | A tilted plane fitted to the outline. | A pile on a slope. |
| **Flat (elevation = 0)** | A plane at elevation 0. | A raster of heights above the ground only. |

### Results

| Result | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cut volume** | The material above the base plane. |
| **Fill volume** | The space below the base plane. It is not shown when the method makes it 0. |
| **Net volume** | Cut minus fill. |
| **2D area** / **3D surface** | The area of the outline on the map, and of the surface of the pile. |
| **Base elevation** | The elevation of the base plane. |
| **Confidence** | For a detected pile: how sure Hub is of the outline. |
| **Weight** | The larger of the cut and the net volume, times the density of the material. |
| **Est. cost** | The weight times the price of the material. |

## Materials, units and currency

The materials are yours. Click **Edit materials…** in the panel to open the **Stockpiles** tab of
the [preferences](/docs/hub-guide/account/preferences). There you change the density and the price
of each material, hide the ones you do not use and add your own. The prices are in the currency
of the **Units** tab. The currency is a label: Hub does not convert prices.

With imperial units, volumes are in yd³, weights in short tons and prices per short ton. Areas
follow the same scale as the [measurements](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx#units). A saved pile keeps the
material it was weighed with.

## Limits & requirements

- **Stockpile Volume** shows when the map has a raster with one band. It is off while the map is split,
  and the [compare view](/hub-guide/analyse/compare-view.mdx) does not have it.
- The address of the page keeps the added layers, not their opacity, order or eye.
- **Edit materials…** needs a signed-in account.
- Saving the pile needs write access to the dataset.
- Hub detects one pile for each click.
