## When to use it

Compare two rasters of the same place to see what changed: two flights on different dates, an
orthophoto and its elevation model, a raster before and after processing. Split the map into
bands, one raster in each, and drag the dividers to swipe between them. Or keep the rasters on top
of each other and change their opacity.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open the dataset and go to the folder with the rasters. Click the **Map** tab. The map shows
   all the files of the folder that have a location.

2. With two or more rasters, the layer opacity panel shows at the bottom. Drag the slider of a
   layer to change its opacity. Use the eye of a layer to hide it and show it again, and **Move
   layer up** and **Move layer down** to change which raster is on top.

   <Screenshot id="analyse/compare-layers--opacity-panel" />

3. Click **Compare georasters side by side** at the top of the map. Each raster gets its own
   band, with its name at the top.

4. Drag a divider to the left or to the right to move the edge between two rasters. You can also Tab to a
   divider and press the Left and Right arrow keys (5 % a step, 1 % with `Shift`), or `Home` and `End`. Use
   **Move left** and **Move right** on the name of a raster to change the order of the bands.

   <Screenshot id="analyse/compare-layers--split" />

5. Click **Compare georasters side by side** again to put the rasters back on top of each other.

</Steps>

## Options

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Opacity slider | The opacity of one raster, from 0% to 100%. With one raster on the map, there is one slider. |
| Eye | Hides a layer or shows it again. Its opacity stays the same. |
| **Move layer up** / **Move layer down** | Changes the order of the rasters. |
| **Add layer** | On the map of one file: adds another georaster of the dataset as a layer, up to 6 in all. **Remove layer** takes it off. See [Map views & saved views](/hub-guide/viewing/map-views.mdx). |
| **Plant Health** | Opens [Plant health](/hub-guide/analyse/plant-health.mdx) for a raster with two or more bands. |
| **Compare georasters side by side** | Splits the map into one band for each raster, with equal widths. |
| Divider | Drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to change the width of the bands next to it. A band is never narrower than 5 %. |
| **Move left** / **Move right** | Moves a raster one band to the left or to the right. The widths of the bands stay. |
| **Compare to another georaster** | Shows when the map has only one raster. On the **Map** tab it opens the raster in its own map, in a new tab, with the file picker to select a second raster. See [Map views & saved views](/hub-guide/viewing/map-views.mdx). |

## Limits & requirements

- The compare button shows on the **Map** tab of a dataset, when the map shows a raster.
- The map compares the rasters of the folder on show. To compare rasters from different folders,
  to save the comparison or to share it as a link, use the [map of a file](/hub-guide/viewing/map-views.mdx).
- **Raster Analysis** and **Stockpile Volume** work with several rasters on the map. With two or
  more elevation models, a selector sets the one they work on: **Raster** in **Raster Analysis**, **Elevation model** in
  **Stockpile Volume**. They are not
  available while the map is split. An opaque raster on
  top hides the colours of **Raster Analysis** on the raster below it: hide it with its eye.
