## When to use it

Set how the Hub shows things to you: metric or imperial units, the default basemap, the default file view,
the quality of splats and more. Your preferences belong to your account. They are the same on every device and
browser where you sign in.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Click the user button at the top right of the Hub, then **Preferences**. The **User preferences** window
   opens.

2. Click a tab and change a setting. The change applies at once, also to the views that are open.

3. Check the line at the bottom: **Saved to your account**. If a save fails, it shows **Not saved,
   retried on the next change**.

4. Click **Close**.

</Steps>

**Reset section** puts the settings of the open tab back to their defaults. **Reset all** puts every setting
back, after you confirm.

A link to any Hub page with `?preferences=<tab>` at the end opens the window on that tab, for example
`?preferences=map`. The tab names are `units`, `map`, `files`, `lists`, `viewers`, `stockpiles` and
`notifications`.

## Units

| Setting | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Measurement units** | **Metric**, **Imperial**: lengths, areas, volumes, weights | **Metric** |
| **Currency** | Any currency, with a search box. Prices, for example the cost of a stockpile | USD |

The measurement units apply to the [measurements on the map](/hub-guide/measure/map.mdx), the
[3D viewers](/hub-guide/measure/3d.mdx), the [splat viewer](/hub-guide/measure/splat.mdx), the distances of
the [elevation profile](/hub-guide/analyse/elevation-profile.mdx) and the stockpile volumes.

A change of currency does not convert prices: 15 USD becomes 15 EUR.

### Custom basemap

Select **Custom** in **Default basemap**. The **Custom basemap** fields open:

1. Select the **Source type**: **XYZ tiles** or **WMS**.
2. Type the **URL**. It starts with `http://` or `https://`. An XYZ URL has the `{z}`, `{x}` and `{y}`
   placeholders, for example `https://tiles.example.com/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`.
3. For WMS, click **Fetch layers** (the refresh button next to **Layer**), select the **Layer** and the
   **Tile size**.
4. Click **Apply**. The Hub loads one tile to check the source. If the tile loads, every map changes to the
   source. If not, an error shows and nothing changes.

**Clear** removes the source. The gear button of a map has the same fields.

## Lists

<Screenshot id="account/preferences--lists" />

| Group | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Datasets | **Datasets per page**: **Fit the screen**, or 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 | **Fit the screen** |
| Datasets | **Open a dataset after creating it** | Off |
| Organizations | **Organizations per page**: **Fit the screen**, or 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 | **Fit the screen** |
| Organizations | **Sort organizations by**: **Name**, **Creation date**, **Last modified**, **Number of datasets** | **Name** |
| Organizations | **Organizations order**: **Ascending**, **Descending** | **Ascending** |
| Organizations | **Show only organizations I own, belong to, or that are public** | On |

With **Fit the screen**, a page holds as many rows as fit the height of the window, from 10 to 100, and the
paginator sits right below them. Hub counts them again when you resize the window. The paginator lists the
number that fits as **N (fit)**. A number that you select stays, and Hub saves it to your account.

## Viewers

<Screenshot id="account/preferences--viewers" />

| Group | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Gaussian splats | **Navigation mode**: **Fly**, **Earth** | **Fly** |
| Gaussian splats | **Quality**: **Auto**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**. Auto lowers the detail on a slow device | **Auto** |
| 3D models lighting | **Ambient**, **Directional**, **Shininess** | 0.4, 0.7, 25 |
| 3D viewer lighting | **Ambient**, **Directional**, **Sky / ground fill** | 3, 1.8, 3 |

See [Gaussian splats](/hub-guide/viewing/splats.mdx) for navigation and quality.

## Stockpiles

The **Materials** that the [stockpile panel](/hub-guide/analyse/stockpile.mdx) uses to weigh and price a pile: for each
material, whether it is shown, its density and its price per tonne. **Add material** adds one of your own.
**Edit materials…** in the stockpile panel opens this tab.

<Screenshot id="account/preferences--stockpiles" />

## Notifications

The e-mails about your builds. See [Build notification e-mails](/hub-guide/account/build-notifications.mdx). The tab is there
only when the server sends e-mail.

## Limits & requirements

- You must be signed in. **Preferences** is in the user menu only then.
