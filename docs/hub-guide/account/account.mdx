## When to use it

The **Account** page shows who you are signed in as, how much storage you use, and how fast you can upload to
the server. From it you change your password and sign out.

## Steps: open your account

<Steps>

1. Click the user button at the top right of the Hub.

2. Click **My Account**. The **Account** page opens.

</Steps>

The page has these cards:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **User Information** | Your **Username**, your **Email** (or **Not set**), the number of **Organizations**, and **Administrator** when you are one |
| **Storage** | The space you use, of the space your plan gives (or **Unlimited**), and an **Upload Files** button |
| **Upload diagnostics** | A test of your connection to the server (see below) |
| **Security** | **Change Password** |
| **Session** | **Logout** |

You cannot change your user name or your e-mail address yourself. Ask an administrator.

## Storage

Your storage is the size of all the datasets of the organizations that you own. Datasets of organizations
that you are only a member of count for their owner. The user menu shows the same use as a percentage. Click
it to open **Storage Info** with the total, the used and the free space.

When no space is left, **Storage Info** shows **No storage left!** and the server refuses new uploads and
imports with **Storage quota exceeded**. Delete files you no longer need, or change your
[plan](/hub-guide/account/plans.mdx).

## Steps: test the upload connection

If uploads are slow, test the connection to the server. The upload window has the link **Uploads slower than
expected? Test the connection**, which opens this card.

<Steps>

1. On the **Account** page, find **Upload diagnostics**. Click **Run test**.

   <Screenshot id="account/account--upload-diagnostics" />

2. Wait. The Hub sends 8 MB of random data to the server, first on one connection, then on six at once. The
   server throws the data away.

3. Read the result: the **Protocol**, the **Round trip** time, **One upload** and **Six uploads at once**,
   in MB/s and Mbit/s. Click **Run again** to repeat the test.

</Steps>

If the server's proxy limits the speed of one upload, a yellow message says so, with a link for the
administrator of the server. If you run the test too often, wait a minute and try again.

## Steps: change your password

<Steps>

1. On the **Account** page, click **Change Password**.

2. Type the **Current Password**, the **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**. When the server has
   password rules, the window lists them from the start and ticks each rule as the new password meets it.
   The eye button in a field shows or hides what you typed; you can reach it with the Tab key.

   <Screenshot id="account/account--change-password" />

3. Click **Change Password**. The button stays enabled: when something is wrong, a message under the field
   says what (a mismatch of the two new passwords shows as you type, a wrong current password shows under
   **Current Password**) and the cursor goes to the first field to fix.

</Steps>

## Limits & requirements

- You must be signed in.
- **Security** and **My Account** are not shown when the server takes accounts from another system (for
  example LDAP). Then change your password in that system.
- Administrators have no storage limit.
