## When to use it

You want a copy of everything you stored on DroneDB Hub, for example before you leave the service or for an
archive. Or you want your account and your data removed.

## Steps: export your data

<Steps>

1. Open a dataset. Do not select a file.

2. Click the download button in the header. Confirm with **Download**. The Hub makes a ZIP of the whole
   dataset. A large dataset becomes a task: the ZIP is ready to download in the
   [Tasks tab](/hub-guide/processing/tasks.mdx) when the task ends.

3. Do the same for each dataset that you want to keep.

</Steps>

The ZIP holds your files as you uploaded them. To get the files that Hub built (Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF, COPC,
GeoPackage, 3D Tiles), right-click a file and use the downloads under **Tools** (see
[Builds](/hub-guide/processing/builds.mdx)). [Download files & ZIP export](/hub-guide/files/download.mdx) explains the other
ways to download.

For a large export, for example a whole organization, write to
[support@dronedb.app](mailto:support@dronedb.app). Say which datasets you want. You get a download link that
works for 30 days.

## Steps: delete files and datasets

- To delete files, see [Folders, rename, delete](/hub-guide/files/folders.mdx).
- To delete a dataset or an organization, see [Delete a dataset / organization](/hub-guide/datasets/delete.mdx). This
  also deletes the files that Hub built from it.

Deleted data stays in the backups of DroneDB Hub for up to 30 days. Then it is removed from every system.

## Steps: delete your account

<Steps>

1. Write to [support@dronedb.app](mailto:support@dronedb.app) with the subject **Account Deletion Request**.

2. Give the e-mail address of your account and confirm that you want to delete it.

3. For 30 days after the request, you can still sign in and download your data.

4. After 30 days, your account, your files and their metadata are deleted for good.

</Steps>

## What happens after the deletion

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| Your data | All your files, datasets and metadata are deleted. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days |
| Organizations you are a member of | Your membership ends. The data of the other members does not change |
| Your subscription | It ends at once. The rest of the billing period is not refunded |
| Recovery | Not possible after the 30 days |

## Limits & requirements

- This page is about hub.dronedb.app. On another server, ask its administrator.
- A server can stop anonymous users from downloading a whole dataset or several files at once. Sign in first.
