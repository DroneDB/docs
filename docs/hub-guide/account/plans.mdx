## When to use it

Read this page to choose a plan for hub.dronedb.app, or to understand a message about a limit. The prices
and the current amounts are on the [plans page](https://dronedb.app/plans).

## What a plan includes

Every plan has every Hub feature: the viewers, the measurement and analysis tools, sharing, OGC and STAC
services, and photogrammetry processing. Every plan has unlimited organizations and datasets. The plans differ
in:

| | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Storage space** | How much data your organizations can hold |
| **Processing priority** | The priority of your processing tasks: best-effort, priority or highest priority |
| **Support** | Community support or priority support |

Each plan starts with a free trial. For more storage or a server of your own, contact
[support@dronedb.app](mailto:support@dronedb.app).

## What happens at a limit

| Limit | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Storage** | The user menu shows your use (**Storage: 5 MB of 40 MB · 12.5%**), amber from 90 % and red from 100 %. **Storage Info** shows **Storage quota reached**. New uploads, imports and tasks that add files fail with **Storage quota exceeded** | Delete files you no longer need, or move to a larger plan |
| **Tasks at the same time** | A new task is refused: **You have too many queued or running tasks; wait for some to finish.** Your organization and the whole server have limits too | Wait for a task to end, then try again |
| **Size of one task** | A task whose result would be too large is refused before it starts. This is a limit of the server for one task, not your storage quota | Process a smaller area or fewer files |
| **ZIP downloads** | **You already have an active download archive being prepared.** | Wait for the archive to finish |
| **Raster export size** | **Export** is grey in the Plant Health and Raster Analysis panels, and the text under it gives the estimate and the limit | Click **Visible extent** to export a part, or download the full COG |
| **A tool that is not in your plan** | The button is grey, or hidden. Its tooltip says why | Contact support |

Your storage counts the datasets of the organizations that you own. See [Account & storage](/hub-guide/account/account.mdx).

## Limits & requirements

- Limits apply to hub.dronedb.app. On a server of your own, its administrator sets them.
